# Narration — locked

Voice: Kokoro `bf_emma`, speed 1.0. One wav per scene; real durations drive scene timing.

## S1 — the exam (hook)

An AI model is graded like a student. It sits standard exams, called benchmarks — and the
scores decide which models the world trusts.

## S2 — the leak (problem)

But a model learns by reading a mountain of text. If the exam questions are already buried
in that mountain, the score measures memory, not ability. That is contamination — and a
score alone cannot reveal it.

## S3 — what Ingot does (product)

Ingot reads the mountain for you. It finds every place the exam's exact words appear in the
training data, and shows you those words in context. Evidence you can judge — not a
percentage you have to trust.

## S4 — how to use it (steps)

Using it takes three steps. Pick the exam. Drop your training data — it is scanned right
here, in your browser tab. Then read the overlaps, with every matching word highlighted.

## S5 — the receipt (close)

Every scan ends with a receipt you can reproduce, and nothing ever leaves your machine.
Ingot. It shows you the words.
