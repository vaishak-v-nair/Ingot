An AI model is graded like a student.
It sits standard exams — benchmarks — and the scores decide which models the world trusts.
But a model learns by reading a mountain of text.
If the exam questions are buried in that mountain, the score measures memory — not ability.
That is contamination — and a score alone cannot reveal it.
Ingot reads the mountain for you.
It finds every place the exam's exact words appear in the training data — and shows you those words in context.
Evidence you can judge — not a percentage you have to trust.
Using it takes three steps. Pick the exam.
Drop your training data — scanned right here, in your browser tab.
Then read the overlaps, with every matching word highlighted.
Every scan ends with a receipt you can reproduce — and nothing ever leaves your machine.
